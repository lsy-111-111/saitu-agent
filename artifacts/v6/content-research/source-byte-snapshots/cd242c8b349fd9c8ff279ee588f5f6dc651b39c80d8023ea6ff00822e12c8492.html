    B. The Contest

    ​

    1. Objective:

    ​

    The Schneider Go Green -  The Schneider Go Green – Energy Tech for Local Impact competition (the “Contest”) aims to foster the development of innovative business solutions that deliver clean and affordable renewable energy to underprivileged communities. This global contest invites students and young entrepreneurs from all geographical areas to propose ideas, solutions, and technologies that enhance energy accessibility and sustainability, thereby improving the quality of life and economic opportunities for those in need.

    ​

    2.  Timeline:

    ​

    The Contest is organized in three rounds, with each round followed by a period of review, assessment, and selection in deciding who will move onto the next round of the competition.  The phases are addressed in further detail below.

    • Phase I – Submission of Project Proposal. Top 10 teams will be selected for the next phase.

    • Phase II – Development and submission of project deck and elevator pitch to showcase an improved design of the initial proposal. Top 3 teams will be selected to pitch to a final jury.

    • Phase III – Digital Competition – Top 3 teams submit their final presentation to jury and winners are awarded.

    II.  Eligibility

     

    A. Individual eligibility

    ​

    1. The Contest is open to university, colleges, and vocational training students worldwide who:

    a) Are between the ages of eighteen (18) and thirty (30) at the time of submission of the Initial Application; AND

    b) Are currently registered and enrolled as full/part-time students in any undergraduate program or graduate program.

     

    B. Team Submission

    ​

    1.  With reference to section III.B.1 below, while registration is on an individual basis. Contest entry is open exclusively to teams consisting of a minimum of two individual registered students, and a maximum of four individual registered students; and

    2. All team members must be studying in the same country.

    a) Team members are not required to attend the same university, nor are they required to study the same subject or be in the same academic year of study.

    b) Students are not required to be permanent residents or nationals of the country in which they are studying to compete.

    3.  We encourage each team to be gender balanced.

    4.  Each individual entrant is allowed to enter the Contest as a part of only one team and to submit only one Initial Application.

    5. The challenge is open globally, but participants should have proficiency in English, as the official language of the challenge will be English.

    2. Entry requirements

    ​

    a) Submissions are deemed to be confidential as set forth in section IX. Intellectual Property B. Ownership and Confidentiality and XI. Confidentiality and must present Participant’s original solution and expertise.​

     

    (1) Make sure to clearly state your objectives and original ideas for a solution that delivers clean and affordable renewable energy to underprivileged communities. Be creative!​

    3.   Important Notices

    • Do not submit ideas that are not your own. Any Applications which are not original to the applicant individual, or team will be subject to disqualification.

    • Do not use ChatGPT tools or similar AI tools. While AI tools like ChatGPT can be utilized for brainstorming purposes, it is crucial that your final submission is distinctly your own. Avoid depending on AI-generated content as the foundation of your idea; instead, use it as a supplementary resource to inspire your original work.

    • Do not use content in your submission that is not yours, or you do not have the authorization to use it.

    • Your idea must not include any confidential information belonging to a third-party.

    • If identical or substantially similar submissions are entered by more than one team, all such submitting teams and submissions may be disqualified from the Contest. 

    • If participants developed the submission as part of a group that included others who are not members of the submitting team, participants should identify any non-submitting team members

    IV. Selection for Phase II


    At the end of Phase I, a jury, composed of Subject Matter Experts, will select the TOP 10 Teams to proceed to the next phase of the Contest. All participants will be informed about their selection status via the contact details provided when entering the Contest.
     

    The selection criteria will include consideration of:


    •    Innovation: does the proposal introduce a new, creative, innovative, unique, and/or compelling solution to the case?
    •    Feasibility: is the proposed idea both technically and economically feasible for implementation?
    •    Environmental impact: How does the solution minimize environmental impact?
    •    Social impact: How effectively does the idea improve the quality of life for low-income communities?
    •  Competitors will also be asked to provide information regarding any attempts by the Competitors to commercialize their ideas/inventions. For example, a Team indicating that they have attempted to commercialize their idea may be asked to provide information about any third parties that have provided investment in the attempt to commercialize the idea or if the Team has provided any third-party with equity in their business or idea.

    ​

    V. Phase II


    A.   Phase II: Top 10 Teams selected for next phase


    If your team is one of the top 10 teams selected by Schneider Electric to proceed to Phase II, the presented idea needs to be developed to a clear evolution of the idea you submitted in Phase I (guidelines will be provided for this submission) and must be sent in PPT format (no longer than 15 slides).  


    B.  Mentoring


    Selected teams may be assigned a mentor from the Schneider Electric team. The mentor will coach the team and help them evolve their idea, and review their CV.


    The mentor’s task is mainly to support and challenge the realization of the team’s solution. Interactions between the mentor and their assigned team will be initiated via the YouNoodle platform; they can be by alternative methods such as phone, email, or Microsoft Teams. The frequency and mode of communication between the mentor and their assigned team will be agreed upon with the mentor, taking into consideration the preferences of both parties. 


    C. Selection to Phase III


    A new jury, composed of Schneider Electric senior leaders, will select 3 of the most promising business cases from the previously selected Top 10 teams to advance to Digital Competition (Finals). All participants will be informed about their qualification status via the contact details provided when entering the Contest.


    The selection criteria will include consideration of:


    •    Adherence to the Official RAC of the Contest.
    •    Innovation: How does the idea introduce a new, creative, innovative, unique, and/or compelling solution related to the case?
    •    Technical feasibility: how feasible is it to implement your proposal from a technical perspective?
    •    Business Case: How clearly is the business case for the idea explained? What revenue streams are proposed, and how realistic a    re they?
     
    VI.  Phase III: Digital Competition


    A.  Phase III: Solution and Business Case Presentation


    All Top 3 Teams selected will be invited to present a final version of the project to a Digital Competition jury, composed of Schneider Electric’s executive leaders who will judge the final presentations.


    Format: The selected teams for Phase III will have to provide support material for a live digital presentation of 30 minutes, which will be followed by a 30 minutes Q&A session with the jury. After the presentations of all finalist teams, the jury will choose one team as a winner, which will be announced at the end of the final day of the event.


    While global considerations may apply in the selection process, all Phase 3 Global- finalist ideas are evaluated on the same basis. The judging criteria at this stage may include:

    ​​

    • Adherence to the Rules of the Contest.

    • Feasibility: how feasible is it to implement this proposal from a business perspective?

    • Business case: How does the financial model ensure long-term viability? What growth opportunities are identified for the future?

    • Solution Innovation: How creative is it compared to currently available solutions?

     
    VII.  Contest Winning Team


    At the end of the final round, the Top 3 winning teams will be given a prize to be split evenly among each team member, pending their adherence to all requirements and obligations for the Competition.


    A total cash prize pool of €10,000 will be awarded as follows:

    ​

    •  1st Prize: €6,000

    • 2nd Prize: €2,500

    • 3rd Prize: €1,500

    ​

    In addition, winners will be celebrated and elevated through a comprehensive promotional campaign designed to enhance their personal brand and visibility.

    ​

    In addition, ongoing mentoring sessions will be provided by leaders from Schneider Electric.
