    What’s in it for me?

    Immerse yourself in a unique learning experience, get hired at l’Oréal & go on a professional mission at l’oréal hq in paris!

    • ALL PARTICIPANTS

      • Get certified and add it on your resume
      • Unlock new skills
      • Fast-track to professional opportunities at L’Oréal
      • Premium access to ADOBE EXPRESS tool

      IMPORTANT: Adobe Express license offer is now CLOSED

    • LOCAL WINNERS

      • Get coached by L’Oréal experts
      • Pitch in-person at the L’Oréal HQ in Paris (travel expenses covered)!

    • MAIN PRIZE

      • Get hired at L’Oréal & go on a professional mission at L’Oréal HQ in Paris!

    Are you in?

    Participate in 3 steps!

    • 1

      Sign up

      On the official L’Oréal Brandstorm platform and receive more information…

    • 2

      Create or join a team

      Create or join an existing team of 3 members and start working on your project!

    • 3

      Submit your project

      Upload slides explaining your idea and create a 3 minutes video presenting your team by answering 5 pre-defined questions.

      Creating your submissions just got easier! We've partnered with Adobe to bring you Adobe Express, the quick and easy create-anything app.

      IMPORTANT: Adobe Express license offer is now CLOSED

    COMPETITION TIMELINE
    ▾

    • Gameplay

      Create a team & submit your idea to enter the competition!

    • Local selection

      Out of all submissions the best teams are shortlisted.

    • Local finals

      Top teams are invited to pitch their ideas at the local L’Oréal HQ!

    • International finals

      Winning teams from participating countries/regions are invited to L’Oréal HQ in Paris to pitch their idea in front of the Executive Jury!

    The exact deadlines depend on WHERE YOU LIVE.
