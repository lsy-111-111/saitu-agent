Frequently asked questions
General
What is the Schneider Go Green Competition?
It's a global challenge for teams of students and young entrepreneurs to develop innovative business models that provide clean and affordable renewable energy solutions for underprivileged communities.
The competition invites teams to develop innovative business models that deliver clean and affordable renewable energy solutions for local communities, aligning with Schneider Electric's mission of promoting youth education and entrepreneurship while enhancing access to energy.
Who are the sponsors of the competition?
Youth Education & Entrepreneurship program sponsorship.
The Go Green Learning Pathway hosted in Schneider Electric University was made possible thanks to an international collaboration between Youth Education & Entrepreneurship program and INCO.
Where can I find the competition registration link?
Registrations are currently closed.
Is there a registration fee?
No, there is no registration fee to participate – it’s free!
How do I know if I have registered successfully for the Challenge?
You will receive a confirmation email within 48 hours (U.S. Eastern Standard Time) of successfully registering for the 2026 Schneider Go Green Competition. This email will be sent to the address you used during registration.
Where can I learn more about the competition?
Information about the go green competition is found on the official competition website www.gogreencompetition.com.
Are students required to travel to participate in the competition?
We are all about sustainability, and making a positive impact on our planet by reducing our carbon footprint. This year we’re taking our commitment to the next level by eliminating the need for travel. The competition will be fully virtual, and students can join us from around the world in their own space.
Who can participate in the competition?
The Contest is open to university, colleges, and vocational training students worldwide who:
a)    Are between the ages of eighteen (18) and thirty (30) at the time of submission of the Initial Application; AND
b)    Are currently registered and enrolled as full/part-time students in any undergraduate program or graduate program.
The challenge is open globally, but participants should have proficiency in English, as the official language of the challenge will be English.
What are the Entry Guidelines for a Team:
While registration is on an individual basis. Contest entry is open exclusively to teams consisting of a minimum of two individual registered students, and a maximum of four individual registered students.
All team members must be studying in the same country.
a) Team members are not required to attend the same university, nor are they required to study the same subject or be in the same academic year of study.
b) Students are not required to be permanent residents or nationals of the country in which they are studying to compete.
We encourage each team to be gender balanced.
Each individual entrant is allowed to enter the Contest as a part of only one team and to submit only one Initial Application.
The challenge is open globally, but participants should have proficiency in English, as the official language of the challenge will be English.
What is the main theme of the challenge?
The theme is "Energy Tech for Local Impact," focusing on identifying a specific energy access, efficiency, or reliability challenge faced by a real community, and design a clean energy solution that directly addresses it. Your solution must be commercially viable, environmentally friendly, and built to last.
What are the key areas of focus within the challenge?
Affordable:
Be financially accessible to low-income users​
Reduce energy costs
Sustainable:
Reduce greenhouse gas emissions​
Use materials that are repairable, recyclable, or sourced responsibly
Innovative:
Incorporate circular economy thinking ​
Where relevant, use digital tools to enhance performance or access
Local:
Give a specific local community access to clean, reliable energy​
Build local skills and create local employment as part of the solution
What types of solutions can participants develop?
Examples include:
Renewable energy source that powers small medium enterprises, supports households in daily activities, lighting for schools, or lighting up pathways for safe commuting.
What impact should our solutions aim to create?
Solutions should provide access to reliable energy, improve wellbeing, increase savings, enhance convenience, boost crop yield/income, improve workspaces for microbusinesses, and offer clean cooking and cooling options.

What are the deliverables for Phase 1?
Participants must identify the problem, propose a solution, and articulate the expected environmental and social impacts.
How will teams be selected for the next phase?
A jury of Subject Matter Experts will choose the Top 10 Teams based on their Phase 1 submissions.
What happens in Phase 2?
Top 10 Teams will develop a detailed business case that justifies their proposed solution, outlining feasibility, benefits, costs, and risks.
What are the requirements for Phase 3?
The Top 3 Teams will present their refined solutions to a jury, including a business case, presentation deck, and a 20-minute virtual presentation followed by a Q&A session.

What are the IMPACT values that participants should embrace?
The IMPACT values are: Inclusion, Mastery, Purpose, Action, Curiosity, and Teamwork are key values to guide the development of meaningful solutions.
How will the winner be determined?
After the final presentations, a global jury of Schneider Electric judges will select one team as the winner, announced via global Schneider Electric social media channels.
What is the Prize for Winning the Go Green Challenge?
At the end of the final round, the Top 3 winning teams will be given a prize to be split evenly among each team member, pending their adherence to all requirements and obligations for the Competition.
A total cash prize pool of €10,000 will be awarded as follows:
1st Prize: €6,000
2nd Prize: €2,500
3rd Prize: €1,500
In addition, winners will be celebrated and elevated through a comprehensive promotional campaign designed to enhance their personal brand and visibility.
In addition, ongoing mentoring sessions will be provided by leaders  from Schneider Electric.
Is the Go Green Competition a job? Or will this lead to guaranteed employment?
No, the Go Green competition is not considered to be a job, and it will not guarantee employment at Schneider Electric
However, upon individual registration to the Go Green competition, registrants will be asked to provide their consent to join our talent community, and understand that we will keep your personal information and track your engagement for 2 years after your last activity, so that you can be considered for job opportunities and contacted by recruiters considering our interests. You can delete your data at any time by logging in and going “Settings” section.
For more information, you can consult Schneider Electric Privacy Notice.
