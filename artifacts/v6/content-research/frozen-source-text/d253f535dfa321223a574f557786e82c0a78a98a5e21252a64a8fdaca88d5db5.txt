Los estudiantes y recién licenciados de todos los cursos y programas relacionados con el diseño

Concept
Mashav - Wearable Ventilation System
University
HIT - Holon Institute of Technology
Holon, Israel
Industrial Design
Student/s
Ofri Shapira
Mashav is a two-piece garment that addresses the
increasing need for comfortable, breathable clothing
as temperatures rise due to the climate crisis. The
garment was inspired by the Galabiya, a traditional
dress worn by Bedouin society in the desert. Its structure
is based on the Chimney Effect, which allows for air
circulation within the garment by utilizing the natural
upward movement of hot air. The hood features a solar
ventilation system that enhances airflow to the neck and
head which are areas with wide blood vessels, leading
to a more effective overall cooling effect. The project
was created with the intention of combining traditional
clothing with modern design, resulting in a functional
and culturally significant garment.
